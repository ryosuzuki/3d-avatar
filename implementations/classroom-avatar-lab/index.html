Gaussian PLY/SPLAT require a Gaussian renderer. The GLB/OBJ are approximate, unrigged meshes. Mixamo does not accept Gaussian PLY.

CAPTURE WELL

Make the whole person visible.

Stand a few steps away, face the camera, keep arms slightly apart, and avoid motion blur. A single photo cannot show the back. For better coverage use a slow turn-around video in 8-view mode. Unseen details are still inferred.

KNOW YOUR ASSET

Gaussian ≠ rigged mesh.

LHM++ can animate through its own body model. An exported PLY does not contain a Unity Humanoid rig. The guide explains your options.

BUILD SOMETHING

Import the static GLB, make a prefab, and place it on a detected plane. Add skeletal animation as a separate, optional step.
