## Interpretation caveats

Our pendulum is an analytic oscillator, not Chalktalk's spring solver. The video is an authored performance; its interactive controls really alter geometry/data, but its shapes and connections are not recognized from live gestures. Natural-looking labels are cursive typography, not handwritten stroke capture. The avatar's skinning and pen grip are approximate adaptations of the supplied Gaussian asset; no supplied skeletal weights or finger rig were available.
