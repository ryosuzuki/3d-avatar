# Orbit Sketch Lab
Audience: spatial computing research colleagues. English narrated 60–100 second actual executable prototype. Thesis: a drawn mark can remain the same object while acquiring depth and behavior. Authoritative assets: exact shared 160,000-Gaussian Ryo avatar and 301,106-Gaussian iPad Scaniverse room from 2026-10-08. Story: draw -> lift the same curves into space -> walk to and tilt a persistent orbit -> accelerate its time -> inspect from another viewpoint. No slide swaps. Authored choreography and analytic orbital paths, not arbitrary sketch recognition, real AI, calibrated AR registration, or accurate solar-system physics.
